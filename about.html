About Max Kitchen

Nigerian flavor, elevated.

MAX KITCHEN brings the generous spirit of Nigerian cuisine to the table through authentic flavors, quality local ingredients and thoughtful fire-led cooking.

In Abeokuta, familiar dishes become a complete dining experience: warm hospitality, polished presentation and the kind of excellent food that makes every gathering feel worth remembering.

We cook to honor where we come from, while creating a setting that feels fresh, intimate and unmistakably our own.
